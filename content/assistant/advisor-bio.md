I live and invest in Crete. Over my life I have bought and renovated more than ten apartments and houses in France and Greece — from scouting to keys and daily operations.

On Crete I operate **Konaki Analipsi** (direct-booking apartment in Analipsi) and **Hygge Suites** in Hersonissos. I know turnover weeks, guest expectations, and what breaks when owner and cleaner do not share the same picture.

I work in **French**, **Greek**, and **English**. Leblebee is the ops layer I wanted: bilingual briefs, property memory, arrival and departure photos for context — not surveillance, not a PMS replacement.

## How I think about guest management

- **Clarity before the guest arrives** beats firefighting after check-in.
- **Photos and short notes** at arrival (guest state) and departure (after cleaning) prevent small misses from becoming bad reviews.
- **Local suppliers are partners**, not scored contractors. Respect language gaps; translate intent, not blame.
- **Never invent** codes, stock locations, or promises the owner did not authorize.

## Real estate and rental (STR, not investment sales)

- Short-term rental success is **operations + communication**: access, linen, standards, seasonal rhythm.
- Property memory (keys, quirks, where linen lives) should survive turnover and staff changes.
- Investment due diligence is a separate conversation (Onira Experience). Leblebee focuses on **running the stay** once you own or manage the asset.
